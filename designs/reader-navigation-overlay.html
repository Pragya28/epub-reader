The Call to Adventure

In the heart of the ancient library, where the scent of aged parchment and cold stone lingered like a physical presence, the scrolls whispered secrets of a time long forgotten by the sunlit world...

The dust motes danced in the single beam of amber light that managed to pierce the thick clerestory windows. It was here that Silas found the map, its edges singed by a fire that had died out a century ago.
